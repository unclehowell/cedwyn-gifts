Gifts

Title and custody of Parcels A, B and C at Tŷ Mawr (Great House), Llandough, 1877 onwards: who held the title and who held the land, side by side, in one chronology. Every transfer recorded as a gift, or with no price stated, is marked, and so is every transfer whose price we have not seen.

Parcels: A = the house parcel (A1 the north woods, A2 the house and garden); B = the meadow; C = the green (village green VG41). Bute and Cardiff Corporation, Daniel Thomas & Son, Mountjoy Ltd, Western Ground Rents, BP and the Williams family are drawn as one continuous band: their hand-offs run into one another with no dividing line. Later holders sit outside the band.

Interpretation

On the records read so far, one transfer is recorded as a gift: Western Ground Rents' gift of about 109 acres of Leckwith Woods to Cardiff council (1940–42), land our boundary measurement places in Leckwith parish, outside Parcels A, B and C. Two steps for the house parcel carry no price as the family's c.1974 statement gives them: the 1877 reversion term and its effect around 1928. 9 grants, transfers or holdings touching the parcels have a price we have not seen. Among them: the 1961 rights granted to Cardiff City Corporation over the north woods; the 1994 transfer of those woods to the Forest of Cardiff, where the register reads “No price recorded” and that year's accounts list no land; and the green, which the 1992 approved plan puts in the community council's care and which the community council now lists among its assets. The 1990 permission enlarged and protected the green by a planning condition; the planning record shows no transfer. The deed named in each row is the record that would settle it.
